Carl von Linné – packing the insert

Eight 40 mm trays under the boards, plus the supply tray C, which rises beside them up to the lid. Everything has its own place; the box can be stored upright.

How to pack

  1. Fill the trays as shown on pages 3 and 4. All cards are sleeved.
  2. Lay the rudder flat in the recess on top of the solo section of tray B.
  3. Click the lids onto the four player trays: press until you feel the click. To open, put a finger in one of the slots on the flower tile side.
  4. Place the trays in the box, back row first: ABC. Then the sample tiles D and the tiles E front left, and the four player trays F–I front right.
  5. Place the boards and the rulebook on the left part, in the order of page 2, with the rulebook on top. Tray C rises beside them at the back right.
  6. Front right, on player trays G and I, there is room for the linen coin bag★, folded.
  7. Put the lid on the box. About 1 mm of space is left, so nothing shifts.
Expansion ★ – not everyone has it. Expansion components are marked with the purple ★ label in this guide:
  • Humboldt: character board and purple wild lens
  • 3 promo voyage cards (Jane Colden, Charles De Geer, Tadeáš Haenke)
  • 6 promo sample tiles
  • 65 metal coins and the linen coin bag

Without the expansion those spaces simply stay empty or less full. The cardboard coins go in the coin compartments of tray C: 1-coins in "coins 1", 2-coins in "coins 2".

With the expansion you play with the metal coins; the cardboard coins stay out of the box. The coins go in tray C; the empty linen coin bag goes folded in the front right of the box.

On top of the trays

The boards and the rulebook lie on the trays on the left. The right side stays open: tray C rises at the back, and the coin bag goes in front.

Order

  1. The 4 player boards first, flat on the trays, against the left wall. They are the thickest (two glued layers) and make a flat base.
  2. The character boards (Linné, Baret, Merian, Buffon and Humboldt★) side by side.
  3. The 4 overview cards.
  4. The game board, folded. It covers the whole stack below.
  5. The rulebook on top: you need it first.
Nothing may lie over tray C: it reaches up to the lid. If the box lid does not close, check whether a board has slid over C, or whether a card or token sticks out above the rim of a tray.
